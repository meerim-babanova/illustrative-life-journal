We took the long way home along the canal because the light was doing that late-summer thing where everything looks slightly gold. Sasha found a paper boat someone had left on a bench and carried it the whole way like it was made of glass.

I didn't want the walk to end, so we stopped for figs at the corner shop that always smells of cardboard and mint, and ate them on the steps until it got cold.
